Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when using our premium travel concierge services. This includes your name, email address, phone number, passport details for bookings, travel preferences, dietary requirements, and payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and elevate our luxury travel services. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Process transactions and send related booking confirmations.
  • Curate personalized itineraries and bespoke travel experiences.
  • Communicate with you regarding VIP deals, concierge updates, and administrative messages.
  • Ensure the utmost security and safety during your international journeys.

3. Information Sharing

We may share your personal information with trusted third-party partners (such as luxury hotel chains, private aviation services, and local exclusive guides) strictly for the purpose of fulfilling your booking. We never sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security

We implement state-of-the-art cryptographic security measures designed to protect your personal information against unauthorized access and alteration. Our VIP database is restricted and monitored 24/7.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. If you wish to exercise these rights, please contact our VIP support team at privacy@chamundatours.com.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Revised" date and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible.
